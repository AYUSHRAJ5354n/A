How to Use

  1. Find the bot on Telegram by searching for your bot username.
  2. Start the bot by sending the /start command.
  3. Send any video file to the bot. It can handle large files (over 20MB) thanks to Telegram's MTProto.
  4. Optionally add a caption with format:
    Title: Your Video Title
    Tags: tag1, tag2, tag3
  5. Wait for the bot to upload your video to Dailymotion.
  6. Receive a link to your uploaded video once it's ready.
